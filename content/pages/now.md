*What I am focused on at the moment. Last updated October 2026.*

I wound down Fimio in May 2026 after four years. Since then I have been building
again: [CodeStories](/2026/10/14/introducing-codestories/) tells a codebase as a
story, with every claim pinned to real lines and every chapter checked like a
test. Ten stories and the full evaluation are public, failures included. The next
version tells the story of a pull request, so a reviewer can follow what a change
does before reading the diff.
