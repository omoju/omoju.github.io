## Experience

**Fimio** — *Founder and CEO*
San Francisco, CA · June 2022 – May 2026

- Founded a venture-backed AI infrastructure company; built and shipped a sandboxed AI inference deployment platform and a malicious smart-contract detection API as a solo technical founder.
- Backed by Redpoint Ventures, Protocol Labs, and Neo, with angel investors including Nat Friedman; wound the company down in 2026 and returned remaining capital to investors.

**GitHub** — *Technical Advisor to CEO*
San Francisco, CA · Feb 2020 – Feb 2022

- Supported the CEO in bringing GitHub Copilot to market, the first mainstream AI coding assistant.
- Led tiger teams prototyping and architecting solutions for the CEO and product leadership, including Gitmos, a real-time visualization of all global open source activity.
- During this tenure GitHub grew from 50M to 73M developers, took Actions to the #1 CI service, and reached 84% of the Fortune 100.

**GitHub** — *Senior Data Scientist, Machine Learning*
San Francisco, CA · July 2017 – Feb 2020

- Applied machine learning to optimize the software development lifecycle on GitHub.
- Co-built GitHub's first repository recommendation engine (Discovery Dashboard), attaining a 20% click-through rate.

**Insight Data Science** — *Data Science Fellow*
Palo Alto, CA · Jan 2017 – June 2017

- Built a large-scale hierarchical classification model for a YC-backed management service company.
- Scaled the solution to accommodate a growing taxonomy of item categories.

**UC Berkeley** — *CSEd Doctoral Candidate*
Berkeley, CA · Dec 2013 – May 2016

- Designed a machine learning research study to investigate gender in intro CS.
- Developed a data science unit within a historical [intro CS course](http://www.whitehouse.gov/the-press-office/2014/12/08/fact-sheet-new-commitments-support-computer-science-education), CS10.

**Google** — *Technology Portfolio Manager*
Mountain View, CA · Feb 2014 – Dec 2015

- Co-led Google's $15M Computer Science Education portfolio, growing it by 25%.
- Co-led the development of the vocabulary glossary for Google's Android Development course.

**ICSI** (International Computer Science Institute) — *Graduate Researcher*
Berkeley, CA · Mar 2011 – Dec 2012

- Built a conceptual metaphor [Semantic Wiki](http://metaphor.icsi.berkeley.edu) capable of ingesting data in English and Farsi.
- Contributed to the design of the conceptual metaphor framework.

**TEDxEuclidAve** — *Founder and Curator*
Berkeley, CA · Aug 2011 – Aug 2012

- Led a 10-person team responsible for executing on the vision.
- Led strategy for [TEDx](http://www.ted.com/tedx/events/3790)EuclidAve, a 100+ member conference bringing together innovative thinkers across technology, design and entertainment in service of social entrepreneurship.

**Center for Advanced Sensors** — *Graduate Researcher*
Memphis, TN · Aug 2003 – Nov 2006

- Co-designed [OntoSensor](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?hl=en&q=Building+a+Sensor+Ontology), a sensor ontology using the Semantic Web Ontology Language (OWL).
- Built an intelligent reasoning agent capable of reasoning with data in XML and RDF formats.

**Thoughtware Learning Technologies** — *Software Engineer*
Memphis, TN · Aug 1999 – Nov 2001

- Quality assurance for an eLearning management system.
